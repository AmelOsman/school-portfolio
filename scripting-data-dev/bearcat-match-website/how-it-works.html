How Bearcat Match Works

Five steps from download to your first study session.

1

Download the app

Get Bearcat Match from the App Store or Google Play on your iOS or Android device.

2

Select your courses

Choose your current courses from the predefined UC BSIT course list to build your match profile.

3

Match with classmates

Bearcat Match connects you with peers enrolled in the same courses who share your availability.

4

Schedule a session

Create or join a study session at a time that works. Groups are capped at 5–10 students.

5

Study on campus or online

Meet at a verified public campus location like Langsam Library, or join a virtual session from anywhere.
