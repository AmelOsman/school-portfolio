About Bearcat Match

About the Project

What is Bearcat Match?

A mobile-first study coordination app that connects UC students by shared courses, availability, and preferred study spots — built by Team 17 as a Senior Design capstone.

The Study Coordination App (Bearcat Match) helps students efficiently form and manage study groups. It connects students based on shared courses, availability, and preferred study locations. Optimized for smartphone use, students can quickly find, join, or create study sessions on campus. The platform supports both in-person and virtual sessions, providing a structured and accessible solution to improve academic collaboration.

The Problem

In 2021, about 61% of undergraduates took at least one class online, and 40% were doing so as of 2023. Online classes keep classmates apart — and face-to-face study provides real-time feedback many students need most.

Our Solution

A campus-based mobile app where students match with peers in shared courses, schedule sessions around their availability, and meet at approved campus locations or virtually — with group sizes kept to 5–10 students.

  • Improve student collaboration and engagement across campus
  • Reduce social barriers that keep study groups from forming
  • Provide a centralized, mobile-accessible coordination platform
  • Support academic success through structured study sessions

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use Bearcat Match?

Any currently enrolled UC student with a valid .edu email address.

How do I get the app?

Bearcat Match will be available on iOS and Android. The app is in active development by Senior Design Team 17 and will launch in early 2027.

Does the app cost anything?

No — Bearcat Match is completely free, developed as a UC Senior Design capstone project.

How big are study groups?

Sessions are capped at 5–10 students, keeping groups focused and easy to coordinate.

Can I study virtually?

Yes. When creating or joining a session you can choose a verified public campus location or a virtual meeting link.

What data does the app collect?

Only the minimum needed: your name, .edu email, and selected courses, plus sessions you create or join. Data is never sold or shared with third parties. See the Policies page for full details.

I have a technical issue. How do I get help?

Use the Help Desk page to submit a support ticket. The team responds within 3 business days.
