01Purpose & Scope

These policies govern use of the Bearcat Match study coordination application, a mobile-first prototype by Senior Design Team 17 at the University of Cincinnati. They apply to every person who creates an account or uses the Platform. Bearcat Match exists solely to help UC students form and manage academic study groups.

02Eligibility & Registration

  • Verified students only. Accounts require a valid .edu email, verified at registration.
  • One account per person with real, accurate information.
  • No shared accounts. Sharing credentials or transferring an account is prohibited.
  • Passwords must be at least 8 characters per UC Password Policy (9.1.23).

03Acceptable Use

Users must use Bearcat Match only for legitimate academic collaboration. The following is prohibited:

  • Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or hate speech.
  • Non-academic solicitation — commercial promotion, advertising, or romantic solicitation.
  • Impersonating another person or registering with false information.
  • Creating fake sessions or joining sessions with no intent to participate.
  • Attempting to probe or bypass the Platform’s security controls.
  • Any activity violating UC policy, the UC Student Code of Conduct, or applicable law.

04Academic Integrity

Bearcat Match supports collaborative studying. It must not be used to share exam questions, complete graded individual work for others, or organize sessions that violate a course’s collaboration rules. The course instructor’s policies always control.

05Study Session Conduct & Safety

  • Approved locations only. In-person sessions at verified public campus locations (e.g., Langsam Library, TUC). No private residences.
  • Virtual sessions use the in-app meeting link.
  • Group size is limited to 5–10 students.
  • Attendance: leave the session in-app if you can’t attend to free the seat.
  • Safety: meet only at the listed public location and use the in-app report tool for unsafe behavior.

06Privacy & Data Practices

The Platform follows data minimization (ACM Code of Ethics, 2018), UC’s Data Governance & Classification Policy (9.1.1), and is designed consistent with FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g).

We collect

Name, UC email, selected courses, and sessions you create or join.

We never collect

Location data, contact lists, browsing history, or anything beyond core features.

No third-party sharing

User data is never sold or shared with any third party.

Retention & deletion

Data deleted at semester’s end unless you opt to retain. Request deletion anytime.

Visibility

Other users see your name and shared courses only. Your email is never shown.

Security

HTTPS everywhere, hashed credentials, OWASP Top 10 (2021), NIST SP 800-63B.

07Accessibility

The Platform targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Accessibility barriers can be reported via the Help Desk and are treated as priority issues.

08Reporting, Moderation & Enforcement

  1. Report received and logged with timestamp.
  2. Team review within 3 business days; session may be hidden during review.
  3. Outcome: no action, warning, session removal, or account deactivation.
  4. Escalation: safety-related reports escalated to UC Public Safety (UC Policy 9.1.8). Call 911 for immediate danger.
Harassment and non-academic solicitation are grounds for immediate account deactivation.
